# Alt D: Playful Character

Frame `sO32Q`, "Orby Website - Alt D - Playful Character", in `~/Documents/Orby.pen` at x 6000, y 40000. It is 1440 × ~11160.

Orby is drawn as a cartoon sticker: a white cut edge, a 4–6 px ink outline (`#1B1035`), a hard offset shadow and blush cheeks. Each section is a solid colour block: brand purple `#2B1A66`, brand orange `#E8704A`, yellow `#FFD23F`, teal `#1EC6A4` and cream `#FFF3E2`. Lavender `#9B7BFF` is used for Usage only. Mood cards use the app's phase colours. Text on light blocks is always ink. Headings use Fredoka 600/700, body text Nunito, hand annotations Caveat and code JetBrains Mono.

## Sections, top to bottom
1. **Nav and hero.** A giant thinking Orby sits on a teal mood disc under a notch status line. Hand-drawn arrows label the motion: eye roll 6980 ms, thought dots 625 ms with a 206 ms stagger, float 4190 ms.
2. **Choreography strip** (yellow). Five keyframe cards follow the scroll ranges from brief §4.4: Ready, Thinking, Working (read), Needs you, Done. Each card has a scroll %, the notch line and a motion note.
3. **Meet Orby** (cream). Seven mood cards, each showing keyframe A → B with a loop length and an easing note. Needs you is the wide card. Below them are 11 tool-prop chips.
4. **Approvals** (orange). The ⌘Y/⌘N key-cap headline sits next to a rebuilt permission card in a notch, with Orby perched on a burst. Three sub-points use cropped real screenshots.
5. **Agents** (teal). Eight orbs in brand colours with a hover/chat annotation, the support table, and the sessions screenshot with the "Jump back" copy.
6. **Usage** (lavender). Screenshot plus three bullets.
7. **Sounds** (dark purple). A singing Orby, seven cue buttons and the "Polite by default" list.
8. **Privacy** (yellow). Three stat stickers and the body copy.
9. **FAQ** (cream). Accordion with the first item open, plus a thinking Orby.
10. **Final CTA** (orange). Done Orby (wink and sparkle) under a small notch.
11. **Footer** (ink). Includes a dozing Orby Easter egg.

## Deviations from the brief
- On the orange Final CTA the primary button is filled purple, because an orange button would disappear on the orange block.
- Screenshots are image fills, cropped by transform from `docs/screenshots/` with a relative URL (`../Project/NotchIsland/docs/screenshots/…`).
